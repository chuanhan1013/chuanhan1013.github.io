## Challenge

The robot had to clear four stages: a ramp, a narrow beam, a water channel and a wind tunnel. It
therefore had to transform between a tracked configuration and an amphibious paddle-wheel
configuration.

## My role

As team lead, I:

- modelled the chassis, gearbox and paddle-wheel mechanisms in SolidWorks;
- 3D-printed them for rapid iteration;
- tuned the drivetrain, centre-of-mass placement and amphibious guidance hardware through repeated
  course tests.

## Outcome

The team was one of only two to complete all four stages and placed 2nd overall under official
scoring.
